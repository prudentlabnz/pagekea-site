PageKea Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Applies to the PageKea app for Android (nz.co.pagekea.app)

The short version

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. PageKea contains no advertising or analytics code.
  • Your documents stay on your phone. PageKea processes files on your device and does not upload them anywhere. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.
  • We don't collect your personal information. We, the developer, never receive your documents, your signatures or anything you type into the app.

Who we are

PageKea is published by Prudent Consultants Limited, a company based in Auckland, New Zealand ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use PageKea, and the choices you have.

Information PageKea does not collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share:

  • your documents, scans, images or their contents;
  • signatures, names, dates or text you add to documents;
  • your contacts, location, photos library, microphone or camera data;
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics.

PageKea declares no internet permission, so the app itself cannot send your data over the network.

To work, PageKea stores some information only on your phone:

Files you choose to save to Documents/PageKea or another shared folder are visible to other apps on your phone that have permission to read your files, just like any other file you save there. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Storage.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include PageKea's settings (listed above, including saved signatures and your name stamp) in that backup, so they come back when you set up a new phone. Your documents are not included. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings; we never see it. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings under Google → Backup.

Features that use Google services

A few features rely on components provided by Google. They are subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (PageKea Pro)

PageKea Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment details; we never receive them. PageKea only receives confirmation from Google Play that Pro has been purchased (and whether it was later refunded) so it can unlock Pro features. Google may share limited order information with us as the seller, such as the order number, date and country, as part of normal Google Play reporting.

Smart scan

"Scan to PDF" uses Google's on-device document scanner, provided through Google Play services. The scanning happens on your phone. If you prefer not to use it, choose Basic camera in the Scan tool, which uses your phone's own camera app.

Text recognition (OCR)

OCR uses Google ML Kit's text recognition model, which is built into PageKea and runs entirely on your device. Your documents are not sent to Google for recognition.

Permissions

  • Biometric / fingerprint — only used if you turn on App lock. Android checks your fingerprint, face or screen lock and simply tells PageKea whether it succeeded. PageKea never receives your fingerprint, face data or PIN.
  • Google Play Billing — lets you buy PageKea Pro through Google Play.

PageKea does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or all your files. When you pick a file, take a photo or choose a folder, you do it through Android's own system screens, and PageKea only gets the item you selected.

Sharing

PageKea only shares a file when you choose to, for example with the Share button, which hands the file to the app you pick (such as email or a messaging app). What that app does with the file is governed by its own privacy policy.

Keeping your information safe

Files in PageKea's private folder can only be read by PageKea. Password-protected PDFs you create use standard PDF encryption; we cannot recover a password you forget. App lock adds a fingerprint, face or PIN check when the app opens.

How long information is kept, and deleting it

  • You can delete any file from the Library at any time.
  • Uninstalling PageKea removes its private folder and settings from your phone.
  • Copies in Documents/PageKea (or a folder you chose) are ordinary files and stay after you uninstall; delete them with your Files app if you no longer want them.

Because we don't hold your personal information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have a question about this, contact us using the details below.

Children

PageKea is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and similar laws elsewhere, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. As described above, we do not hold personal information collected through the app. If you contact us by email, we will use your email address only to reply to you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how PageKea handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
